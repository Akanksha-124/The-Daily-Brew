THE DAILY BREW

A Luxury Boutique Café | Coffee, Kitchen & Little Moments

Act as a world-class brand designer, luxury hospitality website designer, senior UI/UX designer and full-stack developer.

Transform THE DAILY BREW into an exceptionally elegant, sophisticated and premium café brand. Build a complete, functional, responsive café website and food-ordering application.

The website must look like an established luxury café or European boutique coffee house, not a generic restaurant template.

1. BRAND IDENTITY

  • Brand name: THE DAILY BREW
  • Brand positioning: Premium boutique café offering specialty coffee, handcrafted beverages, gourmet snacks, Italian pasta, artisan bakery and exquisite desserts.
  • Brand personality: Sophisticated, Timeless, Minimal, Warm, Elegant, Contemporary, Exclusive but welcoming.
  • Tagline: "A Little Luxury in Every Moment."

2. VISUAL DESIGN DIRECTION

Create an expensive-looking, editorial-style website inspired by luxury European cafés, Parisian coffee houses and contemporary boutique restaurants.

  • Colour palette: Warm ivory (#F7F3EB), Soft champagne (#D9C6A5), Espresso (#30231D), Deep walnut (#49372D), Muted taupe (#A99A88), Soft white (#FFFEFA).
  • Typography: Headings: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display. Body: DM Sans or Manrope. Refined typography, elegant letter spacing and beautiful text hierarchy.
  • Design principles: Generous white space, large editorial photography, minimal navigation, beautiful image compositions, delicate borders and understated shadows, elegant buttons with subtle hover effects, refined transitions and micro-interactions, sophisticated layouts with asymmetrical editorial sections.
  • Avoid: Bright colours, neon gradients, excessively rounded cards, cartoon illustrations, emoji decorations, cheap-looking templates, excessive animations, overcrowded layouts, generic AI-generated food visuals.

3. PHOTOGRAPHY REQUIREMENTS

  • Use authentic, realistic, professional food and café photography.
  • Photography should look like professional restaurant photography taken using a DSLR camera: natural window lighting, realistic food textures, soft natural shadows, warm neutral colour grading, premium restaurant presentation, shallow depth of field.
  • Required: Beautiful cappuccino with detailed latte art; espresso in ceramic cups; iced Spanish latte in transparent glasses; specialty matcha beverages; beautifully plated Italian pasta; gourmet sandwiches and artisan toast; premium cheesecakes and chocolate desserts; freshly baked croissants and pastries; luxury café interiors with natural sunlight; coffee preparation and barista photography.
  • Every product must have its own relevant photograph. Do not reuse the same photograph across different menu products.
